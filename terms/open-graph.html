Open Graph & social cards

Tags that control the preview when a link is shared.

LinkedIn, Slack, iMessage, X and Discord read og:title, og:description and og:image (1200×630 works almost everywhere) to build a card. Without these tags, the preview is a bare URL or whatever image the app guesses.

<meta property="og:title" content="Ada Park · Product designer">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://ada.example/og.png">
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
