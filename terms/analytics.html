Analytics

Measuring who visits and what they do.

Analytics show page views, referrers, countries and devices. Privacy-friendly tools (Plausible, Fathom, Vercel Web Analytics) work without cookies, so no consent banner is needed. GA4 is free and powerful, but it uses cookies and needs consent in many regions.
