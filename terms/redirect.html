Redirect

Sending a request from one URL to another.

301/308 mean the move is permanent, and search engines pass the old page's ranking to the new URL. 302/307 mean temporary. Redirect old URLs when you restructure a site. Otherwise, links people shared and pages Google ranked will turn into 404s.

{ "redirects": [{ "source": "/old", "destination": "/new", "permanent": true }] }
