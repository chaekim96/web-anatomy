Sitemap

A list of every URL you want found.

sitemap.xml lists your canonical URLs with optional lastmod dates. Submit it in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Keep it accurate: only list pages that return 200, aren't noindexed and use canonical URLs.

<url>
  <loc>https://ada.example/work/atlas</loc>
  <lastmod>2026-10-01</lastmod>
</url>

  1. Step 11: Meanwhile: the crawlers. Googlebot and AI crawlers make the same trip. That is everything search results and AI answers have to work with, so SEO and GEO start with the website itself.
