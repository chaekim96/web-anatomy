CDN

Copies of your site in data centers around the world.

A content delivery network keeps your files on edge servers close to visitors, so someone in Seoul doesn't wait for a server in Virginia. It also absorbs traffic spikes and attacks. Most modern hosts put a CDN in front of every site by default.
