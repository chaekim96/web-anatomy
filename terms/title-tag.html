Title tag

The clickable headline in search results and the browser tab.

It's the single most important on-page tag. Make it unique for every page, put the main topic first, and keep it under about 60 characters so it isn't cut off. Google sometimes rewrites titles that look stuffed or vague.

<title>Ada Park · Product designer in Seattle</title>
