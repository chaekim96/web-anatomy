robots.txt

A file at the site root that tells crawlers where not to go.

It's a request, not a lock: polite bots obey it and anyone else can ignore it. It's also where you allow or block AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot) one by one, and where you point to your sitemap.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /drafts

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://ada.example/sitemap.xml

Where it shows up in a page load

  1. Step 11: Meanwhile: the crawlers. Googlebot and AI crawlers make the same trip. They read robots.txt and the sitemap, fetch each page and read the head (title, description, canonical, structured data) and the content. That is everything search results and AI answers have to work with, so SEO and GEO start with the website itself.
