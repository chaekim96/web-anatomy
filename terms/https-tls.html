HTTPS & TLS

Encryption between the browser and the server. It's the padlock.

TLS uses a certificate, issued by an authority such as Let's Encrypt, to prove the server really is example.com, and then encrypts everything in transit. Browsers warn on sites without it, and search engines prefer HTTPS. Hosts like Vercel and Netlify issue certificates automatically.
