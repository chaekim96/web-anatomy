DNS records

The entries in a domain's phone-book listing.

A maps a name to an IPv4 address, and AAAA to IPv6. CNAME points one name at another name. MX says where email goes. TXT holds text, often used to prove you own the domain (for Google Search Console, for example).

example.com.      A      76.76.21.21
www.example.com.  CNAME  cname.vercel-dns.com.
example.com.      TXT    "google-site-verification=…"
