HTTP

The request–response language browsers and servers speak.

A request has a method (GET to read, POST to send), a path, headers and an optional body. A response has a status code, headers and a body. HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 send many requests over one connection, which is why lots of small files are less costly than they used to be.

GET /work HTTP/2
Host: example.com
Accept: text/html

HTTP/2 200
Content-Type: text/html; charset=utf-8
Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate
