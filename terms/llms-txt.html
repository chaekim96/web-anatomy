llms.txt

A proposed Markdown summary of a site, written for language models.

llms.txt is a community proposal, not a standard. It's a /llms.txt file that summarizes the site and links its key pages in plain Markdown. Few AI crawlers are known to read it, so treat it as a cheap extra, not a replacement for good HTML and structured data.
